कुंभ-अ-मित्र Kumbh-a-Mitra

A companion for the mela that keeps working when the network and the crowd don't — find your way, find your family, and call for help even with no signal.

मेले का साथी — रास्ता, परिवार और मदद, बिना नेटवर्क के भी।

Who are you? · आप कौन हैं?

Each person has their own way in. Pilgrims can start as a guest — no sign-up needed for the help button.

I am an admin

What makes it different

Works with no signal Map, routes and Mitra run on the phone. An SOS with no network hops to nearby phones.
Wristbands for people with no phone Children and elders carry a printed code; any volunteer can reach their family.
Crush prediction without tracking Head-counts per landmark per minute — no user column, so no one's movements are stored.
Crowd-aware navigation Live GPS, voice in Hindi, Marathi and English, and a new route the moment a path closes.

Try this first

  1. An alert travels. Open the pilgrim app, the control room's volunteer queue, and a volunteer's task page (sign in with the security key — see server/db/FIRST-RUN-CREDENTIALS.txt). Press the red button, pick Someone is missing, confirm — it lands in the queue within a second. Claim it, then resolve it.
  2. The map re-routes. In the app, tap Sangam Ghat and press Start. In the control room, click a path on the way and mark it Closed — the app says "New route".
  3. No network, no problem. Press the ✈ button in the app and send an SOS: it says waiting to send. Open a second app tab — the alert is carried across.
  4. Ask Mitra. "toilet kahan hai", then "wahan bheed hai?", "koi aur?", "chalo" — it remembers the place and starts walking.
